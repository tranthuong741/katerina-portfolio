De Rao

Building a beverage brand, developing drink menus, and executing communication strategies.

Founder & Manager F&B Menu & Marketing 01/2026 – 06/2026
Project Goal

Establish a retro-style cafe space, formulate handcrafted beverage recipes, and host community workshops to foster engagement among young patrons.

Let's discuss this project ↗

An actual F&B project founded and directly operated by myself.

My Role

  • Founding and shaping brand identity (Brand Concept)
  • R&D and formulating drink recipes (Pure roasted coffee, Matcha, Fruit Teas)
  • Designing retro interior ambience and promotional materials
  • Hosting community workshop series in collaboration with Rowena Tattoo

Strategy & Execution

  • Creating a nostalgic atmosphere with phin coffee filters and books
  • Combining beverage service with experiential activities (henna art, board games)
  • Driving organic word-of-mouth and social media communications

Data & Tools

  • Consistently achieving 550 – 600 monthly orders
  • Average monthly revenue: 15M – 18M VND (~$600 – $720)
  • Canva, CapCut, Microsoft Office, KiotViet for internal accounting

Key Achievements

  • Built a loyal and recurring customer base aged 16–35
  • Created a cultural gathering spot for youth who appreciate retro aesthetics
  • Maintained a stable profit margin and optimized operational overhead

Founder's Perspective

A small beverage shop doesn't require heavy ad spending if it possesses a distinct "soul". Authenticity in space and beverage flavor serves as the most effective key to retaining customers long term.
