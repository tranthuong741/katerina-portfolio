Quick Questions

Why did you choose Brand Marketing?

Because it is where my two greatest interests intersect: understanding consumer psychology and market dynamics, then translating them into brand stories and practical campaigns. This direction aligns directly with my Marketing major while building directly upon my hands-on experiences at Đờ Rao Coffee, Rowena Tattoo Studio, and digital media projects.

What were your specific responsibilities across your projects?

Rowena Tattoo Studio: directly managed multi-channel content (TikTok, Instagram, Threads), produced videos/imagery, and developed media campaigns to drive revenue growth. Đờ Rao Coffee: shaped the vintage concept, designed the beverage menu, managed daily operations, and deployed marketing strategies to maintain stable order volumes. Internal Accounting & Finance: handled internal bookkeeping, payroll calculation, KPI tracking, and cash flow reconciliation.

Do you have experience working with real customers and data?

Yes, I interact with customers and metrics daily through operating a cafe (listening to direct customer feedback, tracking monthly order counts) and managing communications for a tattoo studio. Additionally, my internal accounting work has sharpened my attention to numbers, cash flows, and performance KPIs.

How do you measure your work performance?

I combine quantitative data with qualitative feedback. In business and media, I monitor order volumes, social media engagement rates, and cash flow efficiency. At the same time, I continuously listen to direct customer feedback to refine products, services, and content for maximum market fit.

How do you integrate technology and AI into your workflow?

I use AI as a powerful assistant for document synthesis and rapid information analysis, alongside familiar tools like Canva, CapCut, and Microsoft Office to design marketing assets, edit short videos, and efficiently manage accounting and administrative data.
