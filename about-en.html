Quick Q&A

Why did you choose Brand Marketing?

Because it is where my two greatest passions meet: understanding customer psychology and market dynamics, then translating them into compelling brand narratives and real-world campaigns. This path aligns directly with my Marketing major while building naturally upon my hands-on experiences at Do Rao Coffee, Rowena Tattoo Studio, and digital media projects.

What were your specific responsibilities in practical projects?

Rowena Tattoo Studio: Directly managed multi-channel content (TikTok, Instagram, Threads), produced video/visual assets, and drove growth-oriented communication campaigns. Do Rao Coffee: Shaped the vintage concept, formulated beverage menus, managed daily operations, and executed marketing strategies that sustained stable order volumes. Internal Accounting & Finance: Managed cash flow, payroll processing, KPI tracking, and financial reconciliation.

Do you have experience working with real customers and data?

Yes, I interacted with customers and data daily through operating a cafe (listening directly to feedback, tracking monthly order counts) and managing communications for a tattoo studio. Furthermore, my internal accounting work has honed my rigor regarding numbers, cash flow, and organizational performance KPIs.

How do you measure work performance?

I combine both quantitative data and qualitative feedback. In business and media operations, I monitor order volumes, social media engagement rates, and financial cash flow efficiency. Simultaneously, I actively listen to direct customer feedback to refine products, services, and content for maximum market fit.

How do you integrate technology and AI into your work?

I utilize AI as a powerful assistant for document synthesis and rapid information analysis, alongside familiar tools like Canva, CapCut, and Microsoft Office to design marketing assets, edit short-form videos, and manage administrative-financial data efficiently.
