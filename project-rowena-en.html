"Buy 1 Get 1 Mini Tattoo" campaign attracting international tourists seeking a Vietnamese mark around Vietnam's National Day (Sept 2).

Content Creator & GrowthBrand Marketing08/2026
Research & Campaign Goal

Validate 3 assumptions: foreign tourists' desire for Vietnamese mini symbols, the effectiveness of the B1G1 deal, and campaign timing around Sept 2.

Discuss this project ↗

Executed at Rowena Tattoo Studio branches.

My Role

  • Surveyed international tourists' mindset
  • Designed the B1G1 campaign structure
  • Executed multi-language marketing

Methods

  • Interviews with 15 foreign tourists
  • Mockup flyer & combo price testing
  • Tracked bookings via IG & WhatsApp

Data & Tools

  • 15 direct in-person interviews
  • "Vietnam Heritage" Flash Design set
  • Multi-language Instagram & WhatsApp

Key Results

  • Assumption 1: Foreigners love local symbols
  • Assumption 2: B1G1 drove group bookings
  • Assumption 3: Coverage pre & post Sept 2 worked
Step 1

Three Assumptions, Three Validation Results

AssumptionValidation MethodResultVerdict & Action
1. Tourists want small Vietnamese memory marksQuick interviews with 15 foreign tourists80% liked minimalist VN symbols taking <30 mins executionCreated "Vietnam Heritage Mini" collection (< 5cm)
2. "Buy 1 Get 1" accelerates booking decisionsTested flyer mockups & combo pricingTourists excited to gift the 2nd tattoo to travel companionsSet rule: gifted tattoo complexity ≤ main tattoo
3. Campaign around Sept 2 optimizes guest volumeTracked real booking rate trendsBackpackers stay longer around holiday periodsRan multi-language campaign mid-Aug through Sept

Data collected from direct interviews with international tourists (n = 15) and cross-channel tracking.

Practical Insight

International tourists have short stays and seek unique souvenirs. The Buy 1 Get 1 deal paired with local culture solved the booking decision barrier without unnecessary price discounting.

Key Insight

International tourist feedback: "I wanted a subtle memory of Vietnamese culture on my skin, and being able to gift the second tattoo to my travel partner made the trip even more memorable."

What the Team Implemented
  • →Designed Vietnam-themed flash artwork
  • →Established clear B1G1 campaign guidelines
  • →Streamlined quick consultation for foreign guests
Key Takeaway

Connecting brand offerings with local cultural context and seasonal travel peaks is a sharp strategy to attract international tourists and generate organic word-of-mouth.

Limitations

Campaign is seasonal, requiring strong foreign language readiness among consultation staff and tattoo artists at the studio.
