"Buy 1 Get 1 Mini Tattoo" campaign attracting international tourists seeking Vietnamese cultural marks in Da Nang branch.

Content Creator & Growth Brand Marketing 08/2026
Campaign Goal

Leverage the peak tourism season around September 2nd to attract international travelers with a "Buy 1 Get 1" promotion, positioning Vietnamese cultural heritage through compact mini tattoos.

Website Rowena Tattoo Studio ↗

Actual campaign executed at Rowena Tattoo Studio systems.

My Role

  • Content planning and detailed content strategy
  • Designing visual assets, posters, and banners (Design post)
  • Acting directly as a KOL featured in promotional videos
  • Managing and publishing multi-platform content

Deployment Strategy

  • Tapping into tourists' desire to preserve memories of Vietnam
  • "Buy 1 Get 1" mechanism: gifting a mini tattoo of equal or lesser complexity
  • Omnichannel media coverage throughout pre and post National Day periods

Tools & Platforms

  • Canva, CapCut & graphic design tools
  • TikTok, Instagram Reels, Threads & WhatsApp
  • "Vietnam Heritage" Flash Design collection

Key Results

  • Encouraged backpackers and tourist groups to book swiftly
  • Optimized studio operational capacity during holidays
  • Strong brand amplification through authentic review videos
